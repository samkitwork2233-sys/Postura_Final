POSTURA ROUND 2 — LOCKED LOGIC

READINESS
Good Posture Range: default 50°–70°, user-adjustable within 50°–100°.

Rule:
Good Min <= Current Angle <= Good Max -> READY
Current Angle < Good Min or Current Angle > Good Max -> CONFLICT

LIVE MONITORING
Firmware continues using:
Deviation = abs(Current Angle - firmware baseline)
Deviation <= Threshold -> Good posture
Deviation > Threshold -> Poor posture

Live Threshold is independent from the Good Posture Range and remains 1°–45°.
The app sends live threshold changes as THRESHOLD:<value> to the existing packed firmware.

RECHECK
Only relevant after a readiness conflict.
Recheck uses fresh current-angle readings from BLE and applies the same range rule.
Recheck does not recalibrate or restart the ESP32.
